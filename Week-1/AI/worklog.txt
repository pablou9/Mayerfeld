Yes. If the goal is pure HTML, remove width="600" as well, since—even if it's not technically CSS—it alters the image's presentation via an HTML attribute.
Leave it like this:
<img
    src="..."
    alt="Healthcare professional assisting a person">
This way the <img> element has no style or sizing attributes and fully complies with the requirement to use only HTML.
