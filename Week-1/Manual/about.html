About Vómer

About Us

Vómer Physiotherapy is a clinic specialized in physical recovery, injury prevention and mobility improvement.

Our work is based on understanding the specific needs of each person and supporting them throughout their entire recovery process.

Our Philosophy

We believe in personalized, close physiotherapy oriented towards results. We want our patients to understand their recovery process and actively participate in it.

Our Values

  • Personalized care
  • Professionalism
  • Close treatment
  • Prevention
  • Commitment to the patient
